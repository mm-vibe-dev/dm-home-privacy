dm-home Alexa Privacy Notice

dm-home Alexa is a private smart-home integration used by its owner to control devices and access home status through Amazon Alexa.

Information used

  • Amazon account identifier provided through Login with Amazon
  • Alexa skill requests needed to perform authorized smart-home actions
  • Device status required to fulfill those requests

dm-home Alexa does not sell personal information or use it for advertising.

Authentication credentials, access tokens, and smart-home device credentials are not intentionally exposed publicly.

Data is used only to authenticate the authorized owner, route permitted smart-home commands, and provide requested device status.

For questions or deletion requests, contact the application owner.
